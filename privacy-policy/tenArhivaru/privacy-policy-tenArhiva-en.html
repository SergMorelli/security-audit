✓ Summary: Shadow Archive does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It is an offline application — all functionality runs locally on your device.

The developer of Shadow Archive (hereinafter — "we", "us", "our") respects user privacy and is committed to full transparency regarding data handling practices.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the game Shadow Archive (Тень Архива), distributed via Google Play, and describes what data (if any) is processed when you use the game.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We do not intentionally collect, store, share with third parties, or otherwise process any personal data of users.

Shadow Archive is an offline application. Its core functionality runs entirely locally on your device and does not require an internet connection.

In particular, we do not receive or use:

  • personal data (name, email, phone number, etc.);
  • device identifiers (Advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI, or similar);
  • location data;
  • in-game activity data (all such information is stored exclusively on your device and is not transmitted to the developer);
  • purchase data (the game has no in-app purchases);
  • crash, error, or performance data;
  • any other technical, diagnostic, or analytical data.
📱 Local storage: All saves, progress, and game settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of the game does not include:

  • advertising SDKs (AdMob, Unity Ads, or any others);
  • analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Appsflyer, Amplitude, etc.);
  • social logins;
  • cloud saves;
  • network functions or APIs.
✓ Network requests: The game makes no network requests, except those required for installation and updates via Google Play (these are performed by the Android OS and Google services, not by the developer).

4. Data Processed by Google and Android

⚠️ Please note: When installing, updating, or using the app, Google Play services and the Android operating system may automatically process technical information (such as IP address, device details, crash logs) in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not have access to such data and do not control its collection or processing.

5. Children

Shadow Archive is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and is not intended to collect personal data from any users.

Since the game does not collect data, we do not request or process information about user age.

If features are added to the game in the future that require processing of any data, we will update this Privacy Policy and publish the new version on Google Play and/or within the application.
